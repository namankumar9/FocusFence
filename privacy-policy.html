Privacy Policy — Ultimate Blocker

Last updated: November 11, 2026. Ultimate Blocker was previously called FocusFence – Website Blocker.

Summary

Ultimate Blocker works entirely on your device unless you choose to sign in to sync your settings between browsers. It never collects your browsing history or the contents of the pages you visit. We do not sell or share your data, and there are no ads or analytics.

Without an account

If you don't sign in, everything you set up — your website groups, schedules, breaks and strictness options — is stored only in your browser's extension storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you sign in to sync

Signing in is optional. When you do, the following is sent to our sync server so your settings can be the same on all your devices:

  • Your email address and password, to create and sign in to your account. The password is stored only as a secure hash. If you choose “Continue with Google” instead, no password is sent: Google tells our sign-in provider your email address, name and profile picture address, which are stored with your account. The extension gets no access to your Google account or its data.
  • Your groups: their names, the websites and page rules in them, schedules, break settings and strictness settings. If a group or its breaks have a password, only its hash is sent, never the password itself.
  • Your devices: the name you give each browser, the browser and operating system, the extension version and when it last synced, so you can see your devices and choose which groups apply on which device.
  • Break times and time used, only if you turn on “Share usage across devices” on that device: when each break started and ended, and how many minutes you have spent on each usage-limited group in the current day or hour, so break allowances and usage limits count across your devices. Which sites or pages you visited is never sent.

This data is used only to sync the extension between your own devices. It is never used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Browsing data

The extension checks the addresses of pages you open against your own lists, on your device, to decide whether to block, gray out or hide parts of a page. It does not record your browsing history, does not track you, and does not send the pages you visit anywhere — whether or not you are signed in.

Where your data is stored

Synced data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, in its Tokyo (Japan) region. Account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password reset, are sent through Resend, which receives only your email address for that purpose. If you sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in. We use no other third parties.

How long we keep it

  • Break times and time used are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Your account, groups and device list are kept while your account exists.
  • Signing out keeps your groups in your browser; it does not delete your account.

Your choices

  • You can use the extension fully without an account.
  • You can turn usage sharing off on any device, and remove devices from your account, in the extension's Sync tab.
  • In Firefox, the extension asks for your permission before sending any data, and you can withdraw it in Firefox's add-on settings.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can only read and change its own data. Passwords are never stored in readable form.

Children

The sync service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change what data the extension handles, we will update this page and the date at the top.
