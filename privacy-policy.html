Privacy Policy — Ultimate Blocker

Last updated: November 11, 2026. Ultimate Blocker was previously called FocusFence – Website Blocker.

Summary

Ultimate Blocker works on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to sign in to sync. It never collects your browsing history or the contents of pages you visit. We don't sell or share your data, and there are no ads or analytics.

If you sign in to sync

Signing in is optional. If you do, we store the following so your settings can be the same on all your devices:

  • Your email address and password (the password is never stored in readable form), or, if you sign in with Google, your email address, name and profile picture.
  • Your settings, such as your groups, blocked sites, schedules and breaks.
  • Your device names and types.
  • If you turn on usage sharing: your break times and the time spent on sites you have put a usage limit on.

This data is used only to sync the extension between your devices.

Who else handles it

Synced data is stored with Supabase in Japan. Account emails are sent through Resend. If you sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in.

How long we keep it

Break and usage times are deleted after 7 days. The rest is kept while your account exists.

Your choices

You can use the extension fully without an account, turn usage sharing off, and remove devices from your account at any time in the Sync tab. In Firefox, the extension asks your permission before sending any data.

Security and children

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can access only its own data. The service is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
